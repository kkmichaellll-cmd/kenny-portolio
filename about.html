HELLO, I’M KENNY.

I turn complex ideas into clear, thoughtful digital experiences.

My design journey began in graphic design in 2015 and grew into UI/UX in early 2022. Today, I bring that visual foundation to digital products and websites, combining thoughtful interactions with a strong eye for detail.

MY STORY

An eye for the visual.
A mind for the experience.

2015Graphic designEarly 2022UI/UX designTodayProducts, websites & beyond

I started as a graphic designer in 2015, building a foundation in visual communication: how typography sets a tone, how colour shapes a message, and how a well-considered layout makes an idea easier to understand. That foundation still informs every screen I design.

In early 2022, I transitioned into UI/UX design. My focus expanded from how something looks to how it works for the person using it. Visual craft became part of a bigger picture: understanding needs, shaping journeys and making each interaction feel clear and intentional.

Since then, I’ve worked across different project types, from fintech and SaaS products to e-commerce experiences and websites. Each brings a different challenge: a dashboard that needs clarity, an onboarding flow that needs direction, or a website that needs to tell a stronger story. I bring research, structure and visual thinking together to find the right response.

Today, I work as an independent UI/UX designer and website developer, with AI adding another dimension to how I explore ideas. My approach connects the visual instincts I’ve developed since 2015 with the product thinking I’ve built since 2022. The goal is simple: create work that people can understand, enjoy and use with confidence.
